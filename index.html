Frequently Asked Questions

Your one-stop resource for answers to commonly asked questions. Still can't find what you need? Have a question? Email us and we'll reply within one working day.

What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

While our quotations are free of charge, if you do decide to move with us we do take a 25% deposit which will be non refundable on cancellation. Rescheduling will need to be done in advance minimum (3 hours) allowing us time to rebook you in at no extra cost

Are you insured to cover my goods in transit?

Yes, we are fully licensed and insured for goods in transit and also for public liabilty should the inevitable happen during the move.

Do we dismantle and reassemble furniture and fixtures if required?

Whilst we would expect a majority of your goods to packed, boxed & ready prior to our arrival to ensure an efficient move, we are fully equiped and able to disassemble and reassemble furniture and fixtures should the need arise.

Are/ will there be extra charges for stairs or long carries?

In short, No. We would expect honest information around the move and your final quote will be all inclusive along with a break down of prices and estimated timescales available to you.. Anything left ambiguous or witheld can incur an additional charge.

Will the driver assist with the loading & unloading?

Yes, the driver is a working driver who will assist carrying and packing the van.

What congestion and/or ULEZ charges?

Whilst we will plan the most efficient route prior to the move avoiding ULEZ and/or congestion charges zones, should there be road closures or diversions that require us to enter or leave ULEZ and/or congestion charges zones on the day, you will be notified of this which can account as an additional charge.

Will you organise parking & permits?

Permits are generally bought by the resident from the relevant council, we cannot apply or obtain these. It'd be left down to you to arrange the permit as and if required. Visitor permits should be fine.

Would you quotation be based on an hourly rate or a fixed rate?

Our quotation will be based on a fixed rate as we believe charging the customer on an hourly rate is unjustified should any unforseen circumstances arise or heavy traffic.

Can I ride in the van with the driver?

As much as we'd love to say yes, unfortunately due to insurance and safety regulations, we can't. We'd advice you to arrange your own transport.
